# About

I am Benedek Bencz. On this site I write about Ml, physics, philosophy and music freely. I also share some of my projects and olympiad study material.

 I am building and researching in mesh. Budapest. Nowadays I think a lot about what (especially) ML and theoretical physics research is there still worth doing in this day and age, or consequently what really are the goals of these fields. In the mean time I also work on aerial firefighting optimisations and making digital instruments.
 
Personally I consider myself an existentialist christian. In my free time I do ballroom dancing or I play and listen to music.
